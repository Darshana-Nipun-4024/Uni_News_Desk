Pakistan clinch ODI tri-series with 3-wicket victory over Sri Lanka in final

Pakistan clinch ODI tri-series with 3-wicket victory over Sri Lanka in final. Pakistan defeated Sri Lanka by three wickets in the final on Saturday to clinch the Tri-Nations One Day International (ODI) series, which also featured England.
